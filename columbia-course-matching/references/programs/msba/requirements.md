# MS in Business Analytics (MSBA) — degree requirements

**Data vintage: 2026-2027.** Requirements differ by entry cohort, so find out
when the student entered. The student's degree audit in Stellic applies the right
version automatically and wins over this file whenever they disagree. Say which
year this data is from whenever you rely on it.

The main part of this file is the **Fall 2026 cohort**, from the IEOR "MSBA New
Student Town Hall, Fall 2026" slides. The Fall 2025 cohort is summarized at the end.

## Totals

- 36 credits, normally 3 semesters.
- Core 18 credits + electives 18 credits.
- At least 18 credits in the IEOR department: course codes IEOR, IEME, ORCS, CSOR.
- 15 credits must be taken within Columbia Business School (CBS). The public
  curriculum page still says 12; the program owner of this skill confirmed 15 is
  correct for 2026-2027.
- Electives may come from IEOR, CBS, or other Columbia graduate schools (SIPA,
  GSAS, ...) with approval. See `../ieor-ms/README.md` for the approval rules.

## Rules that constrain a schedule

- Credit limits: 15 per semester in semesters 1 and 2; 12 in semester 3. An
  advisor can raise the limit on request.
- Full-time minimum 12 credits per semester, except a final semester needing fewer.
- No Pass/Fail and no R grade in credit-bearing courses.
- At least B- (2.67) in core courses and overall.
- All courses taken at Columbia.
- F-1/J-1 students: at most one online class counts toward full-time enrollment.
- IEOR E4999 Fieldwork: 1 or 1.5 credits in fall/spring, 2 in summer, 3 total.
- Many CBS courses are 1.5-credit half-term courses (A-term or B-term) or block
  weeks. Two half-term courses in the same slot do not conflict if one is A-term
  and the other B-term.

## Mutual exclusions

- IEOR E4402 and IEOR E4403: not both.
- IEOR E4711, FINC B8306 and MATH G5280: only one.
- More exclusions sit in the `comments` column of the pre-approved CSVs.

## Fall 2025 cohort (differences)

Taken from a Fall 2025 entrant's Stellic audit, read November 2026. Same 36
credits, same 2.67 minimum GPA, but the Business School share is smaller:

- IEOR core (15 credits): IEOR E4101, E4523, E4650 Business Analytics, E4004, E4524.
  E4650 takes the place of DROM B8101 and counts as IEOR credit.
- Additional IEOR credit: at least 3 credits.
- Columbia Business School: MRKT B9651 plus at least 9 credits of CBS electives,
  so 12 CBS credits in total, not 15.
- 0-credit: IEOR E4598, E4599, E4502, and ENGI E4000 (PDL).
- Remaining credits: IEOR or other approved electives up to 36.

Cohorts before Fall 2025 are not covered; use the audit.
